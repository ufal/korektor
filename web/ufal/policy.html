Korektor Spellchecker Plugin Privacy Policy

When using the Korektor service for performing grammar error correction, you send your data to our REST service at https://lindat.mff.cuni.cz/services/korektor.

  • The processed data sent to the REST service might be logged temporarily by various proxies and the service, and the data can be used for further improvements of the service. However, the data will not be published or sold or released.

  • Optionally, when editing the corrections returned by the REST service, the user might choose to send us the corrected text. In that case, the original text, the text produced by the service, and the user-correcte text is sent to the service. Such data can be used for further improvements of the service; however, the data will not be published or sold or released.
